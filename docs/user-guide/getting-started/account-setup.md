# Account & Verification

## Creating an Account

1. Click **Get Started** or **Create Account** on the welcome page.
2. Enter a unique username (minimum 6 characters), valid email address, and secure password.
3. Click **Create Account**.

---

## Email Verification

To ensure security and prevent unauthorized access, **new users must verify their email address before logging in**.

1. After registering, check your email inbox for a verification email from **MealPlan**.
2. Click the **Verify Email** link in the message.
3. Once confirmed, you will be directed to the login page to sign in.

!!! tip "Didn't receive a verification link?"
If you attempt to log in before verifying, a prompt will appear on the login screen allowing you to request a fresh verification link.

---

## Changing Your Email Address

1. Go to **Settings ⚙️** from the navigation menu.
2. Click **✏️ Change Email** next to your current email address.
3. Enter your new email address in the modal and click **Send Verification Link**.
4. Check your new inbox and click the verification link to confirm the change.

---

## Account Deletion

1. Go to **Settings ⚙️**.
2. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** card.
3. Click **🗑️ Delete Account** and confirm the prompt.
4. All personal data, meal plans, shopping lists, and portion storage records will be permanently removed.
