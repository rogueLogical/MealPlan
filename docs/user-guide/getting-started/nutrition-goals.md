# Nutrition Goals & Settings

MealPlan uses your personal nutritional goals to calculate macro targets for every recipe you view, balance, or generate.

---

## Setting Daily Macronutrient Targets

1. Navigate to **Settings ⚙️**.
2. Under **Daily Nutrition Goals**, enter your target daily grams for:
   - **Protein (g)** (4 kcal/g)
   - **Net Carbs (g)** (4 kcal/g)
   - **Fat (g)** (9 kcal/g)
3. Total calories and energy percentage distributions calculate automatically in real time.

---

## Configuring Meal Structure & Splits

- **Number of Meals & Snacks**: Choose how many meals (1–6) and snacks (0–6) you consume daily.
- **Macro Split Sliders**: Adjust the percentage sliders to divide your daily macronutrients between meals and snacks (e.g., 80% for meals / 20% for snacks).
- **Calculated Per-Meal Target**: MealPlan automatically computes the target macros for an individual meal or snack.

---

## Dietary Restrictions & Preferences

- **Dietary Restrictions**: Check applicable tags (Keto, Low-Carb, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Kosher, Halal, etc.).
- **Disliked Foods**: Enter comma-separated ingredients or cuisines you want to avoid. The AI Recipe Generator and Macro Balancer will avoid recommending these items.
