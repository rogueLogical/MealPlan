# Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

## Account & Login

### Why is my login attempt rejected?

MealPlan requires email verification. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to activate your account. You can request a fresh link directly from the login screen if needed.

---

## Recipe Scaling

### Why did the Macro Balancer suggest adding or swapping an ingredient?

If a recipe is structurally missing a macro (e.g., a dish with chicken and oil has zero carbohydrates), mathematical scaling alone cannot reach a carb target. The balancer suggests an ingredient addition (like brown rice) to make the recipe mathematically solvable while preserving culinary quality.

---

## Portion Storage

### Why are low-stock warnings showing on my Home Dashboard?

When a recipe in Portion Storage drops to 3 or fewer remaining portions, a low-stock alert appears on your Home page to remind you to schedule a new prep cycle.
