# Portion Storage & Fridge Tracking

Track prepared recipe portions stored in your fridge or freezer.

---

## Adding Portions to Storage

- **From Meal Plans**: Mark a planned recipe as cooked and log the prepared portions.
- **From Recipe Details**: Open any recipe and click **📦 Add to Storage**.

---

## Recording Consumption

1. Navigate to **Portion Storage 📦**.
2. Click **🍴 Record Eating 1 Portion** on a stored recipe card to deduct a portion.

---

## Low-Stock Warnings

Recipes with **3 or fewer portions remaining** display a yellow/red low-stock warning alert on your **Home Dashboard** so you know when it's time to prep again.
