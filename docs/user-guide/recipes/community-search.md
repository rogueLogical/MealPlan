# Community Recipe Discovery

Explore, favorite, and copy recipes created by the community.

---

## Searching & Filtering

1. Click **🔍 Search Community** in the Recipes view or Home page.
2. Search by keyword or apply dietary filter tags (Keto, High-Protein, Dairy-Free, etc.).

---

## Favoriting & Copying (Forking)

- **Favorite (★)**: Saves the recipe to your **My Favorites** collection.
- **Copy Recipe (📋)**: Clones the public recipe into your personal library, allowing you to edit ingredient weights, instructions, or scale portions for your own prep plans.
